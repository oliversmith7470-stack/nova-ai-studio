Why we built NOVA

We built NOVA because the tools for telling a story in moving pictures were locked behind three things: prices, quotas, and waiting lines. If you had a story but no budget, the door was closed.

We want anyone with a story to be able to make a film — free. We will never put a paywall between a creator and their story.

What NOVA is

NOVA AI Studio is a free AI video-generation studio. You type what you imagine, and you get a real video back. No subscriptions. No daily limits. No queues.

What we believe

  1. A story should never need a budget to be told.
  2. Free means free — no subscriptions, no daily limits, no queues.
  3. The tool should do the hard work, not the creator.
  4. Honesty about limits — free generation, subject to the computing capacity available at that moment, said plainly.

Our goals

  1. Free video generation for everyone, always.
  2. Real cinema quality from a text prompt.
  3. A studio that grows with its creators — more models, longer films, more languages.
  4. Never a paywall between a creator and their story.
