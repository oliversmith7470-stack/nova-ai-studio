Why we built NOVA

We built NOVA because the tools for telling a story in moving pictures were locked behind three things: prices, quotas, and waiting lines. If you had a story but no budget, the door was closed.

We want anyone with a story to be able to make a film — free. We will never put a paywall between a creator and their story.

What NOVA is

NOVA AI Studio is a free AI video-generation studio. You type what you imagine, and you get a real video back. No subscriptions. No daily limits. No queues.
